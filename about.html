About CrafterLady

Your stash, for less

CrafterLady is a small, independent craft supply deals site. Every hook, needle, cotton, and fabric bundle is picked by hand from eBay and Etsy — no bots, no auto-posted feeds. We look for real savings, decent seller ratings, and materials we'd actually put in our own project bags.

Affiliate disclosure

CrafterLady participates in the eBay Partner Network and Etsy's affiliate program. When you click through and make a qualifying purchase, we may earn a small commission — at no extra cost to you. This never affects which deals we choose to feature; it just helps keep the site running.

How we pick deals

Each find is checked against its item's typical price, the seller's rating and return policy, and whether it's still in stock at the time of posting. Prices and availability can change quickly on both marketplaces, so always confirm details on the listing page before you buy.
